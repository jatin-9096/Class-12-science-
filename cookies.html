Cookies & Browser Storage

Shorveer Notes is a free, account-free Class 12 study platform. Here is a plain-English breakdown of the small pieces of data saved in your browser so your reading experience stays smooth.

Last updated: November 2026 · Zero logins · Zero paywalls
01 · Overview

What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are tiny text files a website can place in your browser. Local Storage (`localStorage`) is a modern browser feature that saves simple preferences directly on your device—and unlike cookies, it is never sent back to a server on every page load.

Shorveer Notes relies primarily on Local Storage so features like your chosen reading theme stay consistent when you move between Physics, Chemistry, Mathematics, and English chapters.

02 · Exact Data Stored

What Shorveer Notes Saves on Your Device

Key / Type Storage Purpose Duration
shorveer-theme-name Local Storage Remembers your selected colour palette (Notebook, Chalkboard, Blueprint, Sunset, etc.) across all pages. Until cleared
cookie-consent Local Storage Remembers that you dismissed the cookie notice banner so it does not keep popping up while you study. Until cleared
Third-Party Resources Browser Cache / Cookies External services such as Google Fonts, MathJax (for equations), or advertising partners (if ads are active) may use standard cookies or cache headers to load assets faster or deliver relevant ads. Varies by provider
03 · Privacy Commitment

What We Never Collect or Store

  • No user accounts or passwords: You never have to sign up to read notes, derivations, or practice MCQs.
  • No personal contact data in cookies: We do not store your name, phone number, email, or school details in browser storage.
  • No selling of student data: Your local preferences stay strictly on your own device.
04 · Your Control

Manage or Clear Your Saved Data

You are always in control of what stays in your browser. You can reset your Shorveer Notes local preferences below with a single click, or clear cookies anytime through your browser settings (Chrome, Edge, Safari, or Firefox → Settings → Privacy & Security → Clear browsing data).

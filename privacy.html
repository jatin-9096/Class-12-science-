01

Information We Collect

Shorveer Notes is designed as a learning platform for Class 12 Science students. We aim to keep the amount of personal information we collect as low as reasonably possible.

Depending on the features enabled on the website, information may include:

  • Information voluntarily entered, such as user display names.
  • Basic technical browser preferences like dark or light mode.
  • Study progress and interactive chapter completion records.
Privacy-first approach We do not ask students to provide unnecessary personal information simply to access educational content.
02

Local Storage & Personalization

Some website features utilize your browser's LocalStorage to retain preferences directly on your device.

This includes theme choices or interactive tracking variables. Clearing browser cache or storage removes these locally kept preferences instantly.

03

How Information Is Used

Collected data points are utilized solely to provide a smoother, customized, and responsive learning workflow across sessions.

  • Remembering user visual preferences across pages.
  • Maintaining interactive chapter completion states.
  • Optimizing platform performance for science learners.
04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Shorveer Notes may employ minimal cookies or technical storage keys required strictly for basic functional integrity and theme controls.

05

Third-Party Services

We utilize reliable cloud hosting providers (such as GitHub Pages or similar infrastructures) to deliver fast study pages worldwide. Standard server logs may process basic connection data automatically.

06

External Links

Our notes pages may link out to educational videos, references, or external portals. Once you leave Shorveer Notes, their respective privacy guidelines apply.

07

Data Security

We employ secure HTTPS standards and strict platform hygiene to protect user browsing interactions. Never input sensitive personal passwords or private data on educational resource portals.

08

Students & Younger Users

Shorveer Notes is dedicated strictly to high school science learners. We never knowingly solicit private records from minors or younger students.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This document may be revised periodically to reflect platform expansions or technical upgrades. Visitors are encouraged to check this page periodically.

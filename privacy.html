Privacy Policy

This page explains what information Shorveer Notes may collect, how it is used, how local browser storage works, and how external services and links are handled.

01

Information We Collect

Shorveer Notes is designed as a learning platform for Class 12 Science students. We aim to keep the amount of personal information we collect as low as reasonably possible.

Depending on the features enabled on the website, information may include:

  • Information that you voluntarily enter into the website, such as a display name.
  • Basic preferences such as dark/light mode.
  • Study progress stored by interactive chapter features.
  • Technical information that may be automatically processed by hosting or third-party services when such services are used.
Privacy-first approach We do not ask students to provide unnecessary personal information simply to access educational content.
02

Local Storage & Personalization

Some website features may use your browser's LocalStorage to remember information directly on your device.

For example, the website may store a display name, selected theme, cookie preference, or chapter completion status.

This information is generally stored inside the browser on the device where the website is being used. Clearing browser storage can remove this information.

03

How Information Is Used

Information and preferences may be used to provide a smoother and more personalized learning experience.

  • Display a personalized welcome message.
  • Remember website preferences.
  • Remember interactive study progress.
  • Improve the usability and functionality of the educational website.

We do not intend to use voluntarily entered information for unrelated purposes.

04

Cookies & Similar Technologies

Shorveer Notes may use cookies or browser storage technologies for functionality and preferences.

These technologies can help the website remember choices such as theme preferences, consent settings, or other functionality-related settings.

Cookie control If the website provides a cookie preference panel, you can use it to manage the available optional categories.

Browser settings can also be used to clear or restrict cookies and stored website data. Some website functionality may not work correctly if essential storage is disabled.

05

Third-Party Services

Shorveer Notes may use third-party services for hosting, analytics, embedded content, advertisements, video, or other website functionality.

If such services are enabled, those providers may process certain technical information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

We recommend reviewing the privacy policies of third-party services before interacting with external features.

Any advertising or analytics service should only be listed here when it is actually implemented on the website.

06

External Links

Shorveer Notes may contain links to external websites, educational resources, social platforms, videos, or other services.

Once you leave Shorveer Notes and visit an external website, that website's own privacy policy and terms apply.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, security, or availability of third-party websites.

07

Data Security

We take reasonable steps to keep the website and its associated systems secure.

However, no website, server, application, or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users should avoid entering sensitive information such as passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other confidential information into areas of the website that do not specifically request it.

08

Students & Younger Users

Shorveer Notes is an educational platform intended to provide study material to students.

We aim to avoid unnecessary collection of personal information from students. Users should not submit sensitive personal information through public forms, comments, or external links.

Parents or guardians who have concerns about information associated with a student may contact us using the available contact details.

09

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, technologies, services, or legal requirements change.

When important changes are made, the Last Updated date at the top of this page will be updated.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.

10

Contact Us

If you have a question, concern, correction request, or privacy-related issue regarding Shorveer Notes, you can contact the website team.

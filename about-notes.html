why this framework exists

A good note respects your limited time.

our drafting process

Five steps. Every single chapter.

"Good notes don't add noise.
They condense complexity into crystal-clear logic."
— the Shorveer drafting rule
  1. 01

    Curriculum Scope & Syllabus Mapping

    Every chapter begins by cross-referencing the official CBSE curriculum documents to identify what is examinable, what is marked for conceptual understanding only, and what has been rationalized.

  2. 02

    Modular Breakdown into 5–8 Units

    A huge chapter is impossible to digest in one sitting. We divide each topic into focused, standalone study blocks with one clear job: define, prove, derive, or solve.

  3. 03

    Dual-Layer Fact & Constant Verification

    Definitions, reaction pathways, physical constants, SI units, and chemical conditions are double-checked against latest NCERT editions to ensure total accuracy.

  4. 04

    Revision-First Formatting & Formulas

    Derivations are styled with explicit reasons for each step. Critical laws, equations, and common question patterns from previous-year papers are highlighted prominently.

  5. 05

    Mobile Screen Readability Audit

    The final pass is conducted on a 6-inch phone screen. If a sentence requires re-reading twice, it gets rewritten. Tables and equations wrap cleanly without horizontal friction.

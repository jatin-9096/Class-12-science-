Pillar 01 · Fundamentals

Charge Properties & Coulomb Vectors

Quantisation: $Q = \pm ne$ ($e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$).
Coulomb's Law: $\vec{F}_{21} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{q_1q_2}{r^2}\hat{r}_{21} = -\vec{F}_{12}$.
In dielectric medium ($K$): $F_{\text{med}} = \frac{F_{\text{vac}}}{K}$.

Flip for life analogy → Newton's 3rd Law Pair
flip for wisdom
Life Connection: Mutual Impact
"Just like $\vec{F}_{12} = -\vec{F}_{21}$, no action in life is one-sided." — Jatin Sandilya

Whenever you put effort into the world, the universe pushes back. Resistance and friction are proof that you are moving forward, not that you are failing.

Pillar 02 · Influence

Electric Field & Flux Lines

Field Intensity: $\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q_0} = \frac{kQ}{r^2}\hat{r}$.
Field Lines: Start at $+q$, end at $-q$. Never intersect (unambiguous direction).
Electric Flux: $\Phi_E = \iint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A} = EA\cos\theta$.

Flip for life analogy → Scalar Flux
flip for wisdom
Life Connection: Invisible Aura
"A charge alters the space around it even before another enters." — Jatin Sandilya

Your character and discipline create an invisible field of influence. People around you feel your standards and energy before you even speak a word.

Area Vector Matters: Align with Purpose
Pillar 03 · Balance

Dipole Vectors & Equilibrium

Dipole Moment: $\vec{p} = q(2\vec{a})$ directed from $-q \to +q$.
Axial vs Equat: $E_{\text{axial}} = \frac{2kp}{r^3}$, $E_{\text{eq}} = -\frac{kp}{r^3}$ ($2:1$ ratio).
Torque & Energy: $\tau = pE\sin\theta$, $U = -pE\cos\theta$.

Flip for life analogy → Rotational Force
flip for wisdom
Life Connection: Navigating Chaos
"When external torque rotates you, seek minimum potential energy." — Jatin Sandilya

In a uniform field, the net force is zero, yet torque tries to twist the dipole. In life, external pressures will try to shake your balance. Align your internal vector with your long-term goals to find stability ($\theta=0^\circ$).

Equilibrium in the Storm
Pillar 04 · Symmetry

Gauss's Law & 3 Symmetries

Total Flux: $\oint \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{s} = \frac{q_{\text{enclosed}}}{\varepsilon_0}$.
• Infinite Wire: $E = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon_0 r}$ ($E \propto 1/r$).
• Infinite Plane Sheet: $E = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$ (constant).
• Spherical Shell: Inside ($r < R$) $E=0$; Outside ($r > R$) $E = \frac{kQ}{r^2}$.

Flip for life analogy → Electrostatic Shielding
flip for wisdom
Life Connection: Shield Your Mind
"Inside a closed conductor, electric field is always zero." — Jatin Sandilya

Electrostatic shielding protects sensitive instruments from external lightning storms. Build a mental shield around your goals so negative noise and external opinions cannot perturb your inner focus.

Point Charge & Dipole Comparison
Electric Field vs Distance ($r$)

A point charge field drops as $E \propto \frac{1}{r^2}$, while an electric dipole field decays much faster as $E \propto \frac{1}{r^3}$. Notice the steeper slope of the dipole curve.

Shell
Thin Spherical Shell (Gauss Application)
Field Inside & Outside a Charged Shell

For $r < R$, $E = 0$ (no enclosed charge). At $r = R$, field jumps sharply to $E_{\max} = \frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$. For $r > R$, it falls as $E \propto \frac{1}{r^2}$.

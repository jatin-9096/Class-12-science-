Pillar 1 NCERT Sec 1.2 - 1.6

Electric Charge & Coulomb's Law

Charge Properties: • Quantisation: $Q = \pm ne$ ($e = 1.602 \times 10^{-19}\text{ C}$)
• Additivity: $Q_{\text{total}} = \sum q_i$
• Conservation: In an isolated system, net charge is invariant.
Coulomb's Law in Vector Form: $$\vec{F}_{21} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{q_1q_2}{r^2}\hat{r}_{21} = -\vec{F}_{12}$$ • $k = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \approx 9 \times 10^9\text{ N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}^2$
• In medium: $F_{\text{med}} = \frac{F_{\text{vac}}}{K}$ where $K = \varepsilon_r$.
Superposition Principle: $$\vec{F}_1 = \vec{F}_{12} + \vec{F}_{13} + \dots + \vec{F}_{1n} = \frac{q_1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\sum_{i=2}^n \frac{q_i}{r_{1i}^2}\hat{r}_{1i}$$
Force Ratio in Dielectric: $F_{\text{med}} = \frac{F_{\text{air}}}{K}$
Pillar 2 NCERT Sec 1.7 - 1.9

Electric Field & Flux Dynamics

Electric Field Intensity ($\vec{E}$): • Point Charge: $\vec{E} = \frac{\vec{F}}{q_0} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{Q}{r^2}\hat{r}$
• Unit: $\text{N/C}$ or $\text{V/m}$ | Dimensions: $[M^1L^1T^{-3}A^{-1}]$
• Continuous System: $\vec{E} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\int \frac{dq}{r^2}\hat{r}$
Electric Field Lines (5 Cardinal Rules): 1. Start at $+q$, terminate at $-q$ (no closed loops).
2. Tangent gives direction of $\vec{E}$ at that point.
3. Two lines NEVER intersect.
4. Normal to conducting surfaces at static equilibrium.
Electric Flux ($\Phi_E$): $$\Phi_E = \iint_S \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{A} = EA\cos\theta$$ Unit: $\text{N}\cdot\text{m}^2/\text{C}$ or $\text{V}\cdot\text{m}$ (Scalar Quantity).
Pillar 3 NCERT Sec 1.10 - 1.12

Electric Dipole in Fields

Dipole Moment Vector ($\vec{p}$): $$\vec{p} = q \cdot (2\vec{a}) \quad (\text{Conventionally directed } -q \to +q)$$ Unit: $\text{C}\cdot\text{m}$ | Scalar charge $\times$ Vector length.
Dipole Field Derivation Outcomes ($r \gg a$): • Axial Line: $\vec{E}_{\text{axial}} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{2\vec{p}}{r^3}$
• Equatorial Line: $\vec{E}_{\text{eq}} = -\frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{\vec{p}}{r^3}$
• Golden Ratio: $|\vec{E}_{\text{axial}}| = 2 \times |\vec{E}_{\text{eq}}|$
Dipole in Uniform Electric Field ($\vec{E}$): • Net Translatory Force: $\vec{F}_{\text{net}} = 0$
• Torque: $\vec{\tau} = \vec{p} \times \vec{E} \implies \tau = pE\sin\theta$
• Potential Energy: $U = -\vec{p}\cdot\vec{E} = -pE\cos\theta$
• Stable: $\theta=0^\circ$ ($U = -pE$) | Unstable: $\theta=180^\circ$ ($U = +pE$)
Dipole Falloff: $E \propto \frac{1}{r^3}$ (Faster than point charge)
Pillar 4 NCERT Sec 1.13 - 1.15

Gauss's Theorem & Applications

Gauss's Law Statement: $$\oint_S \vec{E}\cdot d\vec{s} = \frac{q_{\text{enclosed}}}{\varepsilon_0} = \Phi_{\text{net}}$$ Enclosed charge determines total flux regardless of geometry.
3 Continuous Charge Densities: • Linear: $\lambda = dq/dl$ ($\text{C/m}$)
• Surface: $\sigma = dq/dA$ ($\text{C/m}^2$)
• Volume: $\rho = dq/dV$ ($\text{C/m}^3$)
Standard Applications Formulas: 1. Infinite Wire: $E = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon_0 r} \implies E \propto \frac{1}{r}$
2. Infinite Sheet: $E = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}$ (Distance Independent)
3. Charged Conductor: $E = \frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}$
4. Thin Spherical Shell (Radius $R$):
- Inside ($r < R$): $E = 0$
- Surface ($r = R$): $E = \frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0} = \frac{kQ}{R^2}$
- Outside ($r > R$): $E = \frac{kQ}{r^2}$

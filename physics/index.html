Unit I & II · Capacitance & Drift
Why your phone screen registers your finger

Your smartphone touchscreen is an array of tiny capacitors. Your body is a conductor; touching the glass changes the local capacitance, altering the electric field so the controller pinpoint tracks your touch.

B
Unit IV · Electromagnetic Induction
How induction cooktops heat pans without fire

High-frequency alternating current generates a fluctuating magnetic field. By Faraday's Law, this induces powerful Eddy currents in the iron pan base, converting magnetic energy directly to heat via Joule effect ($I^2R$).

n
Unit VI · Wave & Ray Optics
How high-speed internet crosses oceans

Undersea glass fiber cables bounce laser light across continents through Total Internal Reflection (TIR). Light remains trapped inside the higher-refractive-index core because the angle of incidence exceeds the critical angle.

hν
Unit VII · Dual Nature of Matter
Why sunlight generates electricity in solar panels

The Photoelectric Effect in action: incident photons with energy greater than the semiconductor bandgap ($h\nu > E_g$) eject electrons from the valence band to create a continuous potential difference and current.

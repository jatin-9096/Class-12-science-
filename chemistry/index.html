Chapter 01 · Colligative Properties
Why salt clears icy roads & antifreeze protects engines

Adding solute particles lowers a solvent's freezing point (Depression in Freezing Point). Ethylene glycol mixed with water stops car radiators from freezing in sub-zero winters or boiling over in summer.

E°
Chapter 02 · Electrochemistry
How lithium-ion batteries power your smartphone

Inside your phone battery, reversible redox reactions shuttle $Li^+$ ions between graphite and cobalt oxide electrodes, converting chemical Gibbs free energy ($\Delta G = -nFE$) directly into electrical current.

[ML₆]
Chapter 05 · Coordination Compounds
How hemoglobin carries oxygen & cisplatin fights cancer

Blood is red because $Fe^{2+}$ sits inside a porphyrin chelate complex in hemoglobin, binding $O_2$ reversibly. Similarly, square-planar cis-$[Pt(NH_3)_2Cl_2]$ binds tumor DNA to halt cancer cell division.

R-OH
Chapter 07 & 08 · Organic Chemistry
From willow bark to aspirin & kitchen vinegar

Acetylation of salicylic acid transforms a stomach-irritating phenol into Aspirin (acetylsalicylic acid), while controlled oxidation of ethanol yields ethanoic acid—the active sour compound in vinegar.

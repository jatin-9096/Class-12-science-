📚 Notes
Chemical Bonding
  • Ionic Bond: $Na^+ + Cl^- \rightarrow NaCl$
  • Covalent Bond: $H_2O$ structure
  • Metallic Bond: $Cu$ lattice
  • Lewis Structure: $NH_3$
  • VSEPR: $CO_2$ linear
Thermodynamics
  • First Law: $\Delta U = Q - W$
  • Enthalpy: $\Delta H = \Delta U + P\Delta V$
  • Gibbs Free Energy: $\Delta G = \Delta H - T\Delta S$
  • Hess's Law: $\Delta H_{total} = \sum \Delta H$
  • Entropy: $S = k \ln \Omega$
Chemical Equilibrium
  • Equilibrium Constant: $K_c = \frac{[C][D]}{[A][B]}$
  • Le Chatelier's Principle
  • Reaction Quotient: $Q$
  • Temperature effect on $K_c$
  • Solubility Product: $K_{sp}$
Redox Reactions
  • Oxidation Number Rules
  • Half-Reaction Method
  • Standard Electrode Potentials: $E^\circ$
  • Cell Potential: $E_{cell} = E^\circ_{cathode} - E^\circ_{anode}$
  • Electrochemical Series
Organic Chemistry
  • Functional Groups: Alcohol, Ether, Aldehyde, Ketone
  • Reaction Mechanisms: SN1, SN2
  • Stereochemistry: R/S
  • Aromaticity: Huckel's Rule $4n+2$
  • Polymerization: Chain growth
Coordination Compounds
  • Ligands: Monodentate, Bidentate
  • Crystal Field Theory: $d$-orbital splitting
  • Isomerism: Linkage, Optical
  • Spectrochemical Series
  • Octahedral vs Tetrahedral
Spectroscopy
  • UV-Vis: $\lambda_{max}$
  • IR: Functional group peaks
  • NMR: Chemical shift $\delta$
  • Mass Spectrometry: $m/z$
  • Fluorescence
Electrochemistry
  • Galvanic Cell: $E_{cell} = E^\circ_{cathode} - E^\circ_{anode}$
  • Concentration Cell
  • Electrolysis: Faraday's Law $Q = n F z$
  • Corrosion
  • Batteries: Lead-acid, Lithium-ion
